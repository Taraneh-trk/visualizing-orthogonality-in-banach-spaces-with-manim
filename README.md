# Orthogonality in Normed Spaces: An Animated Educational Series

## Overview

This repository contains the source codes and implementations for an educational video series on **different types of Orthogonality in Normed Spaces**.

The main goal of this project is to present concepts from **Functional Analysis** in an intuitive and visual way. The series is designed for students and mathematics enthusiasts who want to learn about normed spaces, inner product spaces, and various notions of orthogonality beyond the classical Euclidean setting.

The animations and visualizations were created using **Python** and the **Manim Community Edition** library.

This project was developed as an undergraduate thesis project under the supervision of **Professor Mohammad Sal Moslehian** at the **Faculty of Mathematical Sciences, Ferdowsi University of Mashhad**.

---

## Topics Covered

The course introduces the necessary background concepts and gradually explores different types of orthogonality in normed spaces.

The covered topics include:

- Introduction to Norms
- From Norm to Metric
- Cauchy Sequences
- Complete Spaces
- Banach Spaces
- Inner Product Spaces
- From Inner Product to Norm
- Hilbert Spaces
- Orthogonality in Inner Product Spaces and its properties

### Orthogonality Concepts in Normed Spaces

The project covers four important types of orthogonality:

- **Roberts Orthogonality**
- **Birkhoff–James Orthogonality**
- **Isosceles Orthogonality**
- **Pythagorean Orthogonality**

For each type of orthogonality, definitions, geometric interpretations, properties, proofs, and examples/counterexamples are presented.
